Movie Rating Check

Hold a modifier key and hover a movie or TV title on any page to see its IMDb, Rotten Tomatoes and Metacritic scores.

OMDb API key

Ratings come from the OMDb API, which bundles IMDb, Rotten Tomatoes and Metacritic in one request. The free tier allows 1000 requests per day. Your key is stored in chrome.storage.sync and is only ever sent to omdbapi.com.

Behaviour

Cache

Results are cached locally for 7 days (misses for 1 day) so repeat hovers don't spend your daily quota.
